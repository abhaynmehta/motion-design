# Style guide: Youthiapa "Mummy ke rules."

- Ground #0E1A15 (deep bottle green — the green Raised Right tee in shadow; not black again). White type and
  white-bordered evidence photos; the orange tee's orange (#FF6B1A) is the marker strikes, the rule numbers and
  "(mostly.)" only.
- Two type families: **Kalam** 700 (Indian Type Foundry, OFL) is Mummy's handwriting — the rules, the numbers, the
  question, "(mostly.)"; **Bodoni Moda** 800 (OFL) is the brand line "Raised Right.", echoing the Raised Right wordmark.
  ₹ comes from Kalam's latin-ext file.
- Type scale: 52 (the "Mummy ke rules:" label, youthiapa.com) · 84 (prices, "(mostly.)") · 120 (the handwritten rules) ·
  180 (Bodoni "Raised Right."). Lines hang from x = 72; rule numbers at x = 72, words at x = 184.
- The device: Mummy's rules handwritten in a list. As each rule is broken, a **printed evidence photo** (a 3:4 footage
  frame on a cream #F4F1EA border, drop-shadowed, tilted a few degrees) slaps onto a pile at (540, 1190), and the rule
  is **struck through in orange marker** (a two-edge felt swipe drawn left to right on a snappy spring). Only the top 3
  photos of a pile show. A pile rises off the bottom when the next one starts.
- Footage: every shot is an "evidence photo" — Youthiapa's own Raised Right campaign film (A, 1920×1080) and Bhuvan Bam's
  sofa film (B, 606×1080). A's crops are shown at ~0.96× (crisp), B's at ~1.29× (playbook L16). 3:4 crops, 780×1040.
- Music drives structure: "In The Night No Control" (1996) — its **dead stop** (song beats 36.5–39) is the parent's
  glare into the lens ("aur ye kya pehna hai?"); the **slam back** (beat 41) is "Raised Right." The vocal pickup brings
  "Raised" in just before the slam brings "Right."
- End card: the Raised Right by Youthiapa logo (lifted from the campaign film's end frame, drawn `lighten` over the
  green), tees ₹1,499, cargos ₹1,999, youthiapa.com.
