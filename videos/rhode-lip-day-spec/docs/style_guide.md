## Type
- Display: Russo One for the site's Rektorat Heavy, lowercase like the site; the moves at 200 px, drawn letter by letter.
- UI: Inter 600 caps +0.12em for the HUD and descriptors, Inter 700 for coach cues, Inter 600 italic for asides.

## Device: a workout class
A deadpan stare in a rounded panel, "never skip / leg day." → the word rolls to "lip". 3-2-1 drop through the panel
like plates. On the drop the HUD builds in (14 set segments, a rolling "move 01 / 14", a clock). Each move: the next film
pushes up through the panel, its word rises and acts out its name on two springs (do, release), the shade's dot and
descriptor, a coach cue. Speed round: hard cuts on every beat, each word does its move in half a beat. The panel then
becomes the class: 14 portraits pop in on 8th notes. The end swings the pencil in under the site's own H1.

## Kinetic vocabulary (per letter, about each letter's 50 % / 85 % point)
lunge: lean forward (skew −16°) and step 70 px · stretch: letters spread from the centre, ×1.1 wide · squeeze: letters
pressed to 62 % width · flex: the "e" bulges ×1.5 tall twice (pump), neighbours pushed aside · twist: alternate letters
skew ±14° one way then the other · lift: letters rise one after another · balance: the word tips 7° then −5° · bend: an
arc · jump: letters hop in turn · lean: skew −20° · move: slides 90 px · press: squashed to 60 % height · push: a
domino shove · spin: each letter flips (mirror and back).

## Rhythm
120 bpm (beat 0.5 s). Hook in the track's bass-less break (b0–8); the drop on b8 starts the class; a move every 4 beats,
then one per beat; the grid pops on 8ths.

## Sound
Mixkit "Swish Swed" from 36.01 s. SFX (synthesised): a roll of ticks into "lip", interval beeps + plate thumps on 3-2-1,
long beep + whistle + boom on the drop, a whoosh per window push, a sound per kinetic action (thump, silk, squeak-pop,
clicks, shimmer), speed-round beeps rising in pitch, plips for the class, chime on the logo, a short whistle on
"same time tomorrow?". −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
