## Type
- Display: Oswald 600 caps for the site's Knockout (condensed gothic, like the brand's "THE BEACHY GOURMAND" headlines).
- UI: Noto Sans 600 (the site's own body face), caps +0.14em for labels.
- Flaps: Oswald 600 — board 46 px, header 92 px, years 250 px.

## Device: a departures board
Split-flap cells drawn on canvas: each steps through its ring (letters, digits, punctuation; years use digits only) one
flap at a time from its key; the top half falls with gravity (s = 1 − u²), the bottom half lands (decelerating), the
turning flap darkens up to 45 %, a hinge line splits every cell. The board cascades row by row; 1948's row flips to
NOW BOARDING in yellow on the PA chime. Each flight: the field wipes in as a circle from where its bottle enters
(bottom left), the bottle lands (heavy spring, 38° → 7°) and later takes off to the top right; the year flaps flip
forward in time; a plane window (cream bezel) shows today's Rio from the brand's film.

## Rhythm
135 bpm (beat 0.444 s). Flights every 8 beats; the six fast ones every beat; the board cascades over 4 beats.

## Sound
Mixkit "Latin Lovers" from the top. SFX (synthesised): flap clatter (tick rolls), a whoosh for every landing and
take-off, an airport ding-dong for NOW BOARDING and for the logo, pops for the window seats, plips for the fleet and the
lineup. −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
