# Style guide: Mokobara "time spent digging: 0 seconds."

- Grounds: near-black #0F0E0D for the hook only (the inside of a black bag); Mokobara yellow #F7C443 for everything
  after the rip. Ink #121110 type on yellow; white + yellow type on black. Yellow is the only accent.
- Footage: Mokobara's own Transit Z films, keyed off the white sweep and baked onto the ground (contact shadows kept as
  a shade of the ground; the packshots keep alpha). Bottom-anchored, full source width: 1.33× (A) / 1.2× (C).
- The beam (hook): a 94 % black veil with a soft circular hole (R 250 → 330) and a warm screen glow; it moves on
  springs (four retargets) and lands on the hands at the zip as the type settles.
- The zip: the only transition. A gunmetal slider crosses the frame on the beat (critical spring, 0.34 s); behind it the
  old page parts in a V (slope 0.55 for the rip, 0.3 after) with a 20 px tape and teeth every 15 px; ahead of it a short
  closed run; then the halves part off. Directions alternate vertical / horizontal.
- Type: Space Grotesk 700 at −0.035 em; Space Mono 700 caps at +0.08 em for compartment labels and the 0 SEC tag.
  Scale 52 / 84 / 168 / 520. Lines hang from x = 72, left-aligned, inside y 269–1536 and clear of the right 140 px.
  Every line rises through its own mask (heavy for heroes, default for labels); the tag grows from its left edge.
- Numbers are heroes: the hook's 2 % and the payoff's 0 are both 520 px; the unit ("seconds.") sits at 84 beside the 0.
