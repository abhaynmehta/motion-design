# Style guide: Minimalist SPF 50 "50 likha. 56.6 nikla."

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ground` | #F96A28 | the field (Minimalist's own orange) |
| `--ink` | #111111 | all type, the lab readout instrument |
| white | #FFFFFF | readout figures, the key number lines ("56.6 nikla.", "₹359") |
The orange fill of the readout bar appears only once the lab figure passes the label's 50.

## Type (a scale, playbook L17)
48 (IBM Plex Mono 600, tracked +0.08 em, uppercase: lab labels) · 72 (Montserrat 800) · 108 (800) · 162 (900) · 300
(900, tabular figures: 56.6). Leading 0.92, tracking −0.035 / −0.05 em. Latin-script Hinglish.

## Picture
Person cut-outs at 1.35× the 608 px source (punch-ins 1.12× on the beat), the squeeze and the close-up in 36 px rounded
windows; the packshot cut-out (MediaPipe magic_touch) for the hook, payoff and end card. Nothing white and empty.

## Rhythm
Tauba Tauba, 95 bpm. Cuts on beats; the readout climbs in sprung steps on the build, freezes (frame and figure) on the
song's dead stop (beat 11), locks on the slam (beat 12). One tighter angle per long take on a beat.
