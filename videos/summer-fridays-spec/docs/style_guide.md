# Style guide — Summer Fridays · "nobody owns just one."

## Palette (measured on summerfridays.com)
| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| beige | #EEEDEC | the site's backdrop: the hook, the shelf, the dots, the end |
| ink | #000418 | type on beige, the logo |
| rose | #B86373 | the one accent: "me:", the strike, "0 self-control.", the ticker, caption chips, the sticker card |
| sand | #D5D1CA | reserved |

## Type
- Display: Jost (variable, 500–800) for the site's Futura PT, lowercase like the site's product names, heavy at −0.045em.
- Accent: Pinyon Script (the site's own script) for "certified" only.
- Caps labels (ticker, flavour shade, price): Jost 600, +0.2em.
- Phone check: nothing that must be read is under 46 px; the YipitData footnote (22 px) is the claim's source line.

## Device: the meme, then the shelf
The hook is a meme layout: caption above, a square photo, caption below. A giant white "1" sits in the wall behind the
woman (her shoulder in front of it) and gets a rose strike. Hard cut to "also me:" and a shelf that fills tube by tube.
Each gateway flavour's tube flies off the shelf (or whips, rises, drops) to the centre; a tube-shaped window grows out of it
into the flavour's texture film; the tube leaves upward and real life follows with a rose caption chip.
The person matte returns for the "#1" (behind the second woman's head) and as a die-cut sticker on the rose card. The
fifteen swatch dots fall away to leave one, then all come back in a ring.

## Rhythm
130 bpm (beat 0.4616 s). Tubes pop every half beat; lifestyle cuts every 1.5 beats; something new at least every 2 s.

## Sound
Mixkit "Cherry on Top" from 46.62 s. SFX (synthesised): a thump on the "1", silk on the strike, a shutter on "also me:",
fifteen pops that climb in pitch (0.80 → 1.45: the self-control draining), a thump on "15 flavours.", whooshes on the
tube reveals, shutters on lifestyle cuts, a boom on "#1", a low pop on the sticker, plips on the swatches, a tick roll as
they return, a chime on the logo. −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
