# Style guide — Rare Beauty · "just a little"

## Palette (measured on rarebeauty.com)
| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| cream | #FDF6F0 | cards (the site's page tint) |
| ink | #000914 | type on cream |
| berry | #7A0036 | the one accent: the full stops, "a gentle reminder:", labels, the wordmark |

| milk | #FFF7E7 | type on footage and on mauve |

## Type
- Display: Ogg Regular, sentence case, −0.02em. 112–150 px.
- UI: Neue Haas Unica Medium, caps, +0.24em: product names, the byline.
- Accent: Rare Script for the two asides that should feel handwritten ("a gentle reminder:", "…okay, two.", "breathe in… / …and out.").
- Words rise through masks and lift out. No fades.

## Device: the dot
Every picture lives in a circle. The full stop of "just a little." is a berry dot that grows into the frame; six faces dab
into a swatch grid; the grid gathers into one circle; the mauve spreads from it like blended blush; that circle breathes
(a slow spring, 2 beats in, 2 beats out); it opens onto Selena; and the last frame closes back into the full stop of
"one dot at a time." 9:16 shots keep their full-frame scale inside a circle (the circle only clips), so an opening circle
never jumps in scale.

## Rhythm
70 bpm (beat 0.857 s). The music thins out on film beat 28, under the end card.

## Sound
Mixkit "Thinking About You" from 24.84 s (a bar line where the groove lifts). SFX (synthesised): water-drop plips for the
full stop and each swatch, silk on circles, a shimmer on the finished cheek, chimes for Find Comfort and the wordmark.
−14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
