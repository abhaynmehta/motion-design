# Kay Beauty: brand facts, references and music

Researched 3 Oct 2026. Everything on screen comes from the brand's own product pages (kaybeauty.com product JSON) or from
the captions of the three reels the client supplied. Nothing is invented; no claim is made for Katrina beyond what the brand's
own campaign images say.

## 1. Brand
- **Kay Beauty**, co-founded by Katrina Kaif and Nykaa (importer: Nykaa KK Beauty Pvt. Ltd.). Sold on Nykaa, Nykaa stores and kaybeauty.com; also Space NK in the UK.
- **Type system (from the site's CSS):** Miegha (high-contrast display serif, roman + italic) and Nesans (fine geometric sans). The film uses exactly these two.
- **Logo:** brush-script "Kay" over a small "Beauty". The site only serves a 141 px PNG; `scripts/prep_logo.py` rebuilds a clean 8x matte from it (no redraw).
- **Colour:** rose-gold and peach packaging, blush sets; the brand's claims cards use a muted rose (#B36C5F), which is the film's one accent.

## 4. Music
- The source reels sit at ~86–90 bpm (smooth, sensual). The cut runs at **120 bpm** for cut density, with a half-time intro under the hook so it keeps that polish.
- **Delivered with an original score** (scripts/music.py): owned outright, so it is safe on a brand account, in paid ads and off-platform.
- **Instagram music:** business and brand accounts only get the **Meta Sound Collection**; mainstream trending songs are not licensed for branded or boosted posts (they get muted or rejected). For in-app music, use the SFX-only render and pick a Meta Sound Collection track at **118–122 bpm** ("chic house", "luxury", "fashion" moods), started on a downbeat. Every cut sits on a 2-second bar line, so any 120 bpm track lands on the edit.
- If it is posted from a creator / collab account (not boosted), the current beauty-GRWM trend is "Ring My Bell" (Anita Ward), per Buffer's September 2026 trend list. It is ~126 bpm, so it drifts slowly against the 120 bpm cut; treat it as a vibe match, not a beat match.
