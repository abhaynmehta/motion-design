# Style guide: Kay Beauty "3 new drops"

## Type
- **Display: Miegha** (the brand's own), roman for statements, italic for the second half of a pair: "3 new / *drops.*", "6 new / *shades.*", "Hydra Base / *Water Crème*". Never all caps.
- **UI: Nesans** (the brand's own), caps with 0.22em tracking, 32–42 px: chapter numbers, claims, labels, chips.
- One left edge at 90 px (100 px inside label cards). Nothing centred. Type stays out of the Reels UI zones (top 14 %, bottom 20 %, right 12 %).
- Every headline rises through a mask on a spring; it leaves upward before the next cut. No fades.

## Colour
- Kay rose **#B36C5F** is the one accent (labels, chips, the rule, the counter). It comes from the brand's own claims card.
- Ink **#2E1E1B** on light footage and porcelain; cream **#FFF8F3** on the dark drips and the purple set.
- Porcelain **#FAF5F2** for cards and split layouts; blush **#F1E2DA** behind the trio card.

## Devices
- **Label card:** a porcelain card at the lower left that grows from its bottom edge and carries every product name (number · line · *italic line*). It holds across two shots like a lower-third.
- **Strips:** three rounded footage strips as the menu ("Pick yours.") and the ask ("Which one first?"); strip 01 opens to full frame.
- **Zoom-through match cuts:** push into a circle (crème, compact), cut, then open pulled-in on the next circle (sponge, bottle mouth).
- **Split:** a porcelain curtain drops to y 760 so a sentence can build line by line while the footage keeps cutting underneath.
- **Count-ups** for the two clinical claims, always with the brand's disclaimer.

## Motion
- Springs only (lib/motion.js): snappy for windows, chips and wipes; default for cards, strips and panels; heavy for photos and big type.
- Hard cuts on beats; flash cuts in the hook on half-beats; the shade flip on half-beats.
- Footage plays at its own speed or in slow motion (0.4–0.8x, frame-blended); normal-speed footage shows real frames only (no blends, no interpolation).
- Grain: six seeded tiles at 10 % overlay, stepped every 2 frames, to bind three different sources together.

## Sound
- Original 120 bpm luxe-house score; hits on the grid; -14 LUFS.
- SFX are soft and physical: thump, water-drop plip, silk, shutter, tick, chime, shimmer.
