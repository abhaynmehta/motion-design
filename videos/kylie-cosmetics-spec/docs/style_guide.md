# Style guide — Kylie Cosmetics · Mood Stones

## Palette (measured on kyliecosmetics.com)
| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| paper | #F8F1F4 | hook and end card (the site's page tint) |
| pink | #EFD7E5 | 8 am card |
| mauve | #B3848F | 3 pm card |
| night | #0E0B0C | 11 pm card |
| deep mauve | #A06674 | the one accent: times in the hook, notes, "obviously." |
| ink | #393939 | type on light cards |
| cream | #FFF8F4 | type on footage and on night |

The day is the colour script: paper → pink (morning) → mauve (afternoon) → night → paper again (the next morning), so the
end loops into the start.

## Type
- Display: TT Chocolates 400 / 400 italic / 600 (the site's face), lowercase, −0.035em. Hook 128 px, clock 210 px,
  act names 100 px demi, mood lines 132 px (italic for the second line).
- UI: Univers LT Std Bold Condensed, caps, +0.22em: notes, end labels, the caption under the wordmark.
- Words rise through masks and lift out (TYPE.rise). No fades.

## Device
- **The stone window.** Every shot lives in a superellipse "pebble" (the bottles are pebbles); its outline is one track
  across five keys (hook, 8 am, 3 pm, 11 pm, end), so it reshapes with each act. It opens to the full frame (an
  overscanned superellipse) and folds back into the next act's card.
- **The clock.** One odometer column of every hour from 8 am to 11 pm: the roll between acts passes through the hours in
  between, and am flips to pm at 12. It sits under the window, so the fold reveals it.
- **Sunrise.** The morning card and the end card arrive as a disc rising from below the frame.

## Rhythm
67.5 bpm (beat 0.889 s). Acts start on beats 6, 14, 22 (strong beats of the bar); cuts every 0.75–1.5 beats inside an act;
something new at least every 1.5 s.

## Motion
Springs only: heavy for type and the wordmark, default for the window and cards, snappy for exits and punches.
Each cut gets a 4–6 % punch-in and a slow 4 % push. Settled positions are whole pixels.

## Sound
Mixkit "Smooth Jazz" from 6.25 s: film beat 0 is the intro's first note, the band enters on beat 2 (the "11 pm" line).
SFX (synthesised): silk on folds, whooshes on opens and sunrises, clock ticks on the roll, a small chime on the wordmark.
Mix −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
