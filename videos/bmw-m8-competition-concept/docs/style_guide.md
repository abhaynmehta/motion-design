# Style guide: BMW M8 Competition "3.2"

- **Type:** BMW Group TN Pro (BMW Type Next). Condensed Bold for numbers and big words, Condensed Light for the timer,
  TN Pro Light for prose lines, TN Pro Regular/Bold caps (0.28em) for labels. Left-aligned at 120 px (16:9) / 90 px (9:16).
- **Colour:** black and white footage-led; the BMW M tricolour (#0060B0 · #003070 · #E02010) is the one accent device:
  the stripe wipes, the small stripe mark before every spec line, the red rule on the drop, the red "M.".
- **Signature device:** the M stripes cross the frame on the three structural cuts (the drop, the breakdown, the lockup).
- **Numbers:** tabular figures; count up on a heavy spring; the hook counter is literal (speed and elapsed time in real time).
- **Motion:** springs only (snappy for type and wipes, heavy for counts); punch-ins on cuts; a short decaying shake on the two drops and "M.".
- **Footage:** real frames only at 1x (no blends), source time quantized to the output frame; letterboxed sources cropped to the active picture.
- **Legibility:** a soft corner scrim (bottom-left) under the counter and spec lines only; grain 9 % overlay and a light vignette bind three sources.
- **Sound:** the track's own grid (100 bpm). Synth SFX: V8 launch rev (two upshifts) into the drop, booms on the drops, whooshes on passes and wipes.
