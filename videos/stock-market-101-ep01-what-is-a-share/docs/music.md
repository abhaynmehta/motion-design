# Music: mellow songs that sit on this edit

The edit is cut to a **76 BPM** grid: every cut, word and SFX lands on a beat or half-beat of a 76 BPM groove. The guide
track in `renders/9x16.mp4` is Mixkit's free "Sweet September" (lo-fi hip hop, 76 BPM). For the post, add the song inside
Instagram, on top of `renders/9x16_sfx-only.mp4`.

film is a pure function of the beat grid, so I can re-time a version to that song's exact tempo.

## How to add it in the Instagram app
1. Upload `9x16_sfx-only.mp4` (the pops, ticks and booms, no music) as a Reel.
2. Add the song, and drag its start so a **kick (the "boom" of the beat) lands on frame 0**. For Jhol, start where the full
   beat is already playing, not on the soft intro.
3. Set the levels to around song 100 % and original audio (the SFX) 30–40 %.
4. Prefer songs marked with the ↗ trending arrow in the music picker, ideally ones still under ~50k Reels (rising, not peaked).

## Account type matters
Business accounts get a restricted music library (mostly Meta's royalty-free Sound Collection). Keep the page as a
**Creator** account to get the full library of popular songs.
