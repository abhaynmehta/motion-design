# Style guide: Bummer "underrated views."

- Ground #0C1E4A (deep night-sky navy) so the scenic waistband "views" glow like lit windows; one accent, sky-cyan
  #58C7FF (the viewfinder ticks, place labels, the wordmark, bummer.in). White postcards carry the product.
- Device: a window. Each trunk packshot arrives as a white-matte **postcard** with cyan **viewfinder** corner ticks —
  "a view." The scenic printed waistband (Bummer's real product detail) sits at the postcard's middle (per-trunk `fy`
  anchor). Below it, the trunk's real name (Archivo 120) + the view it is (DM Mono 44, e.g. NORTHERN LIGHTS) + a recall
  kicker (scenic waistband · modal-soft). Four swap in, one per four-beat bar, on the four-on-the-floor kick; labels only
  show on the settled card. On the song's big kick they gather into a 2×2 wall of views.
- Footage: Bummer's own product packshots (1254 px), cover-fit into the postcard at ≤0.7× — always a downscale, so crisp
  on a phone (playbook L16). No video, no upscale, no frame blend.
- Type scale: 44 (DM Mono labels, bummer.in) · 72 (Modal Stretch Trunks) · 120 (hook, view name, payoff, ₹599) · 160
  (the bummer wordmark). Archivo 900 for heroes at −0.02 em; DM Mono 500 tracked +0.14 em for the postcard-stamp labels.
  All type sits y 300–1510 (inside the Reels safe area).
- Motion: springs only. Postcards slide up into place (settle spring) and slide up and out when the next arrives; the
  wall pops in staggered; everything leaves upward before the end card rises.
