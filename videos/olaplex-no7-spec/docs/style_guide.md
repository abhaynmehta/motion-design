# Style guide: OLAPLEX Nº.7 "humidity vs your hair"

## 1. Palette
| Token | Hex | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `--ground` / `--bg` | #0C0B0A | a warm near-black for a night-match broadcast (olaplex.com is #000 / #FFF) |
| `--ink` | #FFFFFF | site type |
| `--mute` | #B9B2A7 | scoreboard labels, footnote |
| `--accent` | #F2B33D | the oil's amber: Nº.7, "wins again.", "3 drops.", "wins.", LIVE / FULL TIME, price |
| plate | #191714 | scoreboard and lower-third plates |
Amber only ever sits on the dark ground or a plate, never on footage.

## 2. Type
- Display: Geist 800/900, tight tracking (−0.035 / −0.045 em), sentence case with full stops.
- UI: Geist Mono 600, uppercase, +0.02 em: scoreboard, footnote, price line.
- Sizes: supers 120–230 px, stat labels 76 px, footnote 26 px (legal; small_ok in script.json).

## 3. Rhythm
Abracadabra preview, 126 bpm (beat 0.4762 s, first beat 0.316 s). Cuts on beats; each replay is 7 beats; the payoff sits
on the song's biggest lift at 16.51 s (beat 34). Nothing holds still longer than 1 s before the CTA (gate L13).

## 4. Transitions
Hard cuts on beats · the after's spring takeover (panel → full screen) · lower-third plates that grow from the left ·
type rising through masks · odometer score digits. Never: crossfades, spins, glitches.

## 5. Device
A live match broadcast: one scoreboard plate (HUMIDITY – YOUR HAIR, ● LIVE → FULL TIME) for the whole match, replays in
a panel under it, broadcast lower thirds for the two big lines.
