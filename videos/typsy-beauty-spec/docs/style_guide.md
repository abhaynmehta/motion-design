## Type
- Display: Poppins 600 (and 600 italic for the second word of each dessert), lowercase like the site's product names, −0.03em.
- UI: Assistant 700 caps, +0.18em: notes, labels, the receipt.
- ₹ from Poppins' Devanagari subset (unicode-range).

## Device: a café menu
The hook's full frame slides up to reveal tonight's dessert menu. Each row has a looping thumbnail; a blush highlight
walks down the rows (one spring per move); the picked dish's thumbnail opens to the full frame for its montage and folds
back into its row with a pink tick. Then the page lifts away for the bill: a receipt with a torn edge types out the
punchline. The end serves the three bottles like the menu's dishes.

## Rhythm
95 bpm (beat 0.632 s). The music's full arrangement lifts on film beat 4, on "…but you can wear it." Cuts every 1–1.25 beats
inside a dessert.

## Sound
Mixkit "Pop 05" from 22.73 s. SFX (synthesised): a plip on the chocolate pour, shutters on the hook cuts, pops for menu rows
and the punchline, clicks for the highlight, whooshes on opens, silk on folds, plips for ticks, a receipt-printer tick run,
a chime on the logo. −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
