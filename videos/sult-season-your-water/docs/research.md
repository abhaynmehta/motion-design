# SULT: brand and competitor research

Done by web search on 3 Oct 2026. drinksult.com and most retailer pages are blocked from the build container, so these facts come from search results and press coverage (sources at the bottom). Anything that goes on screen is marked ✅ when it comes from SULT's own copy or press interviews, and ⚠️ when it needs the brand's confirmation.

## 1. The brand in one paragraph
SULT™ is a UK electrolyte drink mix: zero sugar, all six essential electrolytes, sold in 4.5 g sachets. It is built for "the in-between consumer", people who care about their health but also have a beer on a Friday, not for endurance athletes. It soft-launched in April 2025 and sold out in hours with no paid media. About seven months later it was in 150 Boots stores, on the shelf Boots made for brands that grew online ("Trending on Social"), with no investors.

## 3. Who they compete with (UK shelf)
| Brand | How they position | Weakness SULT can use (never named on screen) |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquid I.V.** (global, sugar-based) | "hydration multiplier", fast hydration | **11 g sugar** per serving |
| **LMNT** | keto / endurance, "salty" | **1,000 mg sodium**, about £1.27 a stick in the UK, built for heavy sweaters |
| **Humantra** | clean everyday powder, about £0.85 a serving | 1,000 mg sodium, sweeter sports-drink taste |
| **Heights Hydrate⁺** | brain-boost hydration (citicoline), cocktail flavours, £29 for 24 (~£1.20) | supplement-led, a "nootropic" story |
| **Dioralyte / Hydralyte** | medical rehydration (illness) | pharmacy/medicine feel |
| **Precision Fuel & Hydration, SiS GO Hydro, High5 Zero** | athletes, tablets, sweat-matched sodium | gym / race-day only |

**White space SULT owns:** *the middle*. Daily (not race-day), zero sugar (unlike sugar-based mixes), mid sodium (500 mg, half the "salty" brands), all six electrolytes, natural colours, made in the UK, built in public with its community. Its own line, "not too sulty, not too sweet", is a Goldilocks position, so the film says it without naming anyone: **not a sports drink, not a sugar rush, not a salt lick: just right.**

## 4. Category context (for the pitch, not the film)
- UK electrolyte and sports hydration market: about £680m in 2026, growing about 9% a year.
- TikTok hydration searches rose about 364% year on year (Aug 2024 to Jul 2025), with roughly another 95% forecast into 2026.
- Gen Z treats electrolytes as the new morning ritual. Hydration has moved from sport into everyday wellness, which is exactly SULT's lane.

## 5. What SULT's audience responds to
Founder-led, honest and warm, a bit cheeky, low-polish but premium. Built in public ("you shaped 60% of this"). Pre-launch FOMO and gamified reveals. So the film should feel like a confident, playful product film, not a gym ad: punchy type, real product, colour and texture from the flavours, and a wink in the copy.
