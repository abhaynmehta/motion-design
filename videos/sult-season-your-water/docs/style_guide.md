# Style guide: SULT spec reel ("Season your water.")

Source material (supplied by the user from drinksult.com): `assets/brand/sult-logo-lime.png` (transparent, 2000 px),
`assets/site/src/*.webp` (product cards, bottles page, subscribe ad). Cut-outs in `assets/site/`.

## 1. Palette (measured from the supplied files)
| Token | Hex | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `--lime` | #D7FC00 | logo file; UI pills/buttons #CCFF00 — the ONE accent |
| `--ink` | #141414 | site nav logo / button text |
| `--teal` | #00BFC2 | box face (measured #01BEC2–#01C7C6) |
| `--teal-deep` | #0B8F96 | box shadow side / sachet shading |
| `--paper` | #E5E3DD | site canvas |
| `--card` | #FFFFFF | site product cards |
| flavours | WB #A1326A / bottle #EE8FD6 · PC #D28F7C / bottle #F4B49A · MT #E4BF7E / bottle #F2D98A | sachet stripes, box pills, bottles |
Flavour colours appear only as the product and its world (backgrounds of the flavour shots); type accent stays lime.

## 2. Type
- Archivo variable (wdth 62–125, wght 100–900) — matches the site's heavy grotesk ("SUBSCRIBE & SAVE") and its UI grotesk.
  Display: 800–900 weight, wdth 78–100, tight tracking. UI: 600–700, tracked caps for labels.
- Left-aligned at x = 90 (9:16), sentence case with full stops (SULT's habit: "season your water.").
- Must-read ≥ 28 px; display 120–240 px. Accent (lime) on the key word only.

## 3. Rhythm
119.87 BPM measured, bar = 2 s. Hook lines on beats 0/1/2, something new every ≤ 1 bar, flavour whips every half bar,
stop-time at b33.5, logo on the drop b34.

## 4. Transitions
Strike-through + collapse · card rise · tear · push-through into the glass · whip with overlap (flavours) · fan ·
hard cut on the bar. Never crossfades, spins, glitches, light leaks.

## 5. Camera
Micro push on every hold (1.00 → 1.04); the glass push-through; a slow drift on product shots.

## 6. Texture & finish
Clean, bright, glossy (their photography). Depth from soft contact shadows, never glow. No grain.

## 7. Text in / out
Words rise through masks (heavy) and lift out; struck lines collapse; pills grow out (snappy, opacity snaps in ≤ 4 frames).

## 8. Sound
Kit synth at 120 BPM (Fmaj7 · G · Em7 · Am): intro → full → dark under facts → full → build → gap → drop on the logo.
SFX on every hit (pop / shutter / thump / whoosh / click / tick) + a sachet rip and a powder fizz. -14 LUFS.
