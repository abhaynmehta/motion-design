### Type
- **Headlines:** ITC Franklin Gothic — bold/demi, ALL CAPS, tight leading. Free stand-ins: _Libre Franklin_ (800/900) or _Archivo_ (800).
- **Body/UI:** **Chivo** (Google Font) 400/600/700.
- **Logo:** custom chunky, blocky, heavy wordmark "SULT" with ™ — square-cornered, almost pixel/stencil feel. Use the PNG; don't recreate it.

### UI / layout motifs
- Pill-shaped buttons (fully rounded), lime with black text, or black with white text.
- Floating pill nav bar in white, rounded, sitting over imagery.
- Rounded-corner cards (≈16–20px radius) on stone background.
- Lime highlighter rectangles behind key words in headlines.
- Horizontal scrolling marquee with strikethrough words.
- Accordions with +/– icons.

### Photography style (key to the motion look)
- **Extreme close-up, flash-lit, high-contrast portraits** — people biting into fruit (raspberries, lemon, mango) with juice spraying, scrunched faces. Very editorial / Dazed-style.
- Hero: fisheye/through-the-glass shot of a face looking into a water glass.
- Product: clean studio packshots on white; bottles on pastel sky-blue backgrounds with condensation.
- UGC: vertical iPhone videos of real people in kitchens mixing sachets (casual, Instagram-story text overlays).

## 5. Formula & claims

**Six electrolytes:** sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, chloride, phosphorus — "research-driven electrolyte ratios".

**Key ingredients (as described on PDP):**
- **Pink Himalayan Salt** — sodium; helps the gut absorb water
- **Potassium** — keeps water inside cells; muscle & nerve function
- **Magnesium (citrate)** — energy, muscle function, reduces tiredness
- **Coconut Water Powder** — plant-derived potassium & trace minerals
- **Calcium (Aquamin)** — marine algae from the North Atlantic seabed, 70+ trace minerals
- **Phosphorus** — part of ATP, energy production

**Free from:** sugar, artificial sweeteners, artificial preservatives, caffeine, fillers, GMO, gluten, dairy. Natural flavours & colours from fruit, vegetable & flower extracts. Keto + fasting friendly.

## 8. Video concepts (build these in Remotion/your motion stack)
**A. Hero pitch film — "SEASON YOUR ___" (20–30s, 9:16 + 16:9)**
Typewriter cycles: SEASON YOUR **MORNING.** / **WORKOUT.** / **3PM SLUMP.** / **HANGOVER.** / **WATER.** — each word with a flavour-coloured flash-portrait cut (fruit-bite style), sachet tear → powder pour → swirl in glass → lime marquee wipe (~~SUGAR~~ ~~CAFFEINE~~ ~~FILLERS~~) → "1,900mg. 6 electrolytes. 0g sugar." → logo + "season your water."

**B. "Built Different" data animation (15s)** — the comparison table as racing bars: sugar bars drop to 0g for SULT; electrolyte bar shoots to 1,900mg. Teal sachet vs grey generic sachet (mirrors their us_vs_them shot).

**C. Six-electrolyte explainer (6 × 5s or one 30s)** — each ingredient pops out of the sachet as a lime-highlighted card with its one-line role (§5).

**D. "When do I SULT?" clock (15s)** — dial spins through FIRST THING → LOW ENERGY → PRE/POST WORKOUT → HANGOVER.

**E. Subscription gift ladder (15s)** — delivery boxes stack up, unlocking Bottle → TINNY → £5 → £10.

**F. Flavour drops (3 × 6s)** — per flavour: colour flood, fruit splash, sachet spin, flavour name in Franklin Gothic caps.

**G. UGC branding kit** — 1s logo sting, caption style (Chivo bold, lime highlight), end card with ★4.9 · 1M+ sachets.

**Pitch-reel structure (what to send them, ~45–60s):** 3s cold open (A's typewriter) → 10s A → 8s B → 8s C → 6s F → 5s G sample → end card: "Motion concepts for SULT™ by [your studio]".

**Motion language:** snappy, punchy cuts on beat; hard wipes in lime; highlighter-box reveals behind words; strikethrough animations; marquee loops; splash/liquid sims; slight camera shake on fruit bites; flash-photo pops. Avoid soft corporate easing.

**Formats:** 1080×1920 (Reels/TikTok, primary), 1080×1350 (feed), 1920×1080 (site hero / pitch).
